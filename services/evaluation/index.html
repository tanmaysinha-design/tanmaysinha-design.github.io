FREQUENTLY ASKED

Clear answers for admissions teams.

Need a more specific answer? Talk with us.

Is TruEnroll a NACES or AICE member evaluation agency?

No. TruEnroll is an enterprise software platform, not a manual credential evaluation agency. We do not issue third-party evaluation certificates for external use. Instead, we provide the automated infrastructure that allows your internal university admissions team to process, translate, and evaluate international transcripts instantly based on your own institutional rules.

How long does the custom integration process take?

Because higher-ed IT departments are constantly backlogged, we don't leave the heavy lifting to you. Every institutional deployment is managed by a dedicated TruEnroll Integration Engineer. We map the software to your exact Slate, Salesforce, Banner, or PeopleSoft data fields, completing full end-to-end testing within 4 to 6 weeks.

How does the engine handle low-quality scans or smartphone photos?

Legacy OCR software breaks down when documents are skewed or blurry. TruEnroll uses context-aware vision logic to read multi-page documents identically to a senior human evaluator. The engine flags illegible pages or missing text elements instantly, ensuring no corrupted or unverified data ever enters your CRM.

Will utilizing this platform disrupt our existing registrar rules?

Absolutely not. TruEnroll enforces strict policy sovereignty. Your registrar has absolute control over the conversion backend. You can customize baseline country profiles, adjust specific credit-hour weights, and override any automated output manually to ensure total compliance with internal campus policies.
