FREQUENTLY ASKED

The questions teams ask before moving faster.

Clear answers regarding policy enforcement, system integration, and security compliance.

What is credential evaluation, and how does TruEnroll automate it?

Credential evaluation converts academic records from any education system into standardized US equivalents. TruEnroll reads transcripts in 50+ languages, extracts courses, credits, and grades, normalizes them to a US GPA and credit equivalency, checks each document for fraud, and returns a structured result in minutes instead of days.

Do you handle domestic transfer credit as well as international evaluation?

Yes. TruEnroll supports both international credential evaluation and US domestic transfer credit articulation, mapping incoming coursework to your program requirements so transfer decisions are fast and consistent.

What is primary source verification, and which countries do you cover?

Primary source verification confirms a credential directly against the issuing authority or official record. TruEnroll supports primary source verification across India, China, Nigeria, and other geographies.

Course-by-course or document-by-document: what is the difference?

Course-by-course evaluation breaks a transcript into individual courses with US credit and grade equivalents. Document-by-document evaluation assesses each credential as a whole when a full course breakdown is not required.

How does TruEnroll detect fraudulent or tampered documents?

Every document receives an automated forensics audit that detects tampering, manipulation, and inconsistencies, alongside institution and accreditation checks. Altered transcripts and unrecognized schools are flagged before a decision.

Does TruEnroll integrate with Slate and other CRMs?

Yes. TruEnroll has native integrations with Slate, Salesforce, Oracle PeopleSoft Campus Solutions, and Ellucian Banner / Recruit, and a Partner API to connect with any other CRM or SIS.

Is TruEnroll secure and compliant?

TruEnroll is built for strict data environments with ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type 2, and ISO 27701 certifications and FERPA, CCPA, and GDPR compliance, plus automated audit trails, role-based access, and encryption at every step.
